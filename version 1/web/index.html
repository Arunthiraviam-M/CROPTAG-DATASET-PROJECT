🌾 About CropTag

Bringing AI-powered food recognition to everyday kitchens

What is this?

CropTag identifies 38 varieties of grains, millets and pulses from a single photo, instantly showing what it is and its nutrition profile. The image is processed locally through the project's Flask inference server.

How it works

A custom-built convolutional neural network, trained from scratch on a labeled image dataset, identifies the grain, millet or pulse from an uploaded image. The trained V2 model is served through a local Flask API, while the web interface displays the prediction and nutrition information.

Why it matters

From traditional millets to everyday pulses, understanding what's on your plate — and its nutritional value — helps build healthier, more informed food choices.
